Homegrown is a community athletics platform for athletes, coaches and organizers in Legazpi City, Albay, Philippines. This policy explains what personal information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can remove it.

Information we collect

Account information — everyone

When you create an account we collect and store:

  • Your first name, last name and display name
  • Your email address
  • Your barangay (the district of Legazpi City you select)
  • Your role — athlete, coach or organizer
  • A profile photo, if you choose to add one

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google profile photo from your Google account. We do not receive your Google password.

Additional information by role

RoleAlso collected
Athlete Sports played, playing position, years of experience, height and weight, a short bio, and whether your profile is public or open to recruitment
Coach Coaching level, years of experience, team or organization name, coaching bio, and any certifications you list
Organizer Phone number (required), organization name and type, sports organized, certifications, and a verification document (see below)

Height and weight are treated as health and fitness information. They are optional to keep accurate and are shown on your athlete profile.

Organizer verification documents

If you register as an organizer, you may upload a photo as proof of legitimacy — for example a barangay certificate, a business permit, or a photo of yourself with identification. This image can contain government-issued identification.

It is stored separately from all other images and is not visible to other users. Only you and the administrator reviewing organizer applications can access it. It is retained while your organizer account exists and is deleted when you delete your account.

Content you create

  • Photos you add to your profile portfolio
  • Team logos, if you are a coach
  • Match results, statistics and ratings recorded from events you take part in
  • Events you create, if you are an organizer
  • Notifications sent to you within the app

Location

The app asks for location permission only on the venue locator screen, and uses it only as the starting point for driving directions to a venue you tap.

Your location is never stored. It is held in the device's memory while the screen is open, sent to Google's Directions service to calculate a route, and discarded. It is not written to our database and is not shared with other users. Declining the permission still lets you browse venues — the app falls back to the centre of Legazpi City.

Crash reports

We use Firebase Crashlytics to record diagnostic information when the app crashes: the error and its stack trace, your device model, operating system version, and the state of the app at the time. This helps us fix faults. It does not include the content of your profile.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your date of birth or age
  • We do not collect payment information — the app has no payments
  • We do not use advertising or analytics tracking, and we do not build advertising profiles
  • We do not access your camera, contacts, microphone, or files other than images you choose
  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone

How your information is used

  • To create and operate your account and sign you in
  • To show your profile to other users of the app, according to your visibility settings
  • To run events, record match results, and calculate statistics, ratings and leaderboards
  • To let coaches find athletes and send team invitations
  • To verify organizers before allowing them to publish events
  • To send you in-app notifications about invitations, events and results
  • To diagnose crashes and improve reliability

Who can see your information

Other signed-in users can see your name, role, barangay, profile photo, sport, position, experience, bio, portfolio photos, statistics, ratings and leaderboard position. If you are an athlete, this includes your height and weight. Your email address and, for organizers, your phone number are stored but not displayed on your public profile.

Only you and an administrator can see your organizer verification document.

Where your data is stored

Your information is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure operated by Google, which may process and store it on servers outside the Philippines. Google maintains its own security and privacy protections for this data.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time, in two ways:

  • In the app: Profile → Settings → "Delete my account"
  • On the web: submit a deletion request, which you can use even if you have uninstalled the app

Deleted immediately and permanently: your name, email address, phone number, barangay, bio, height and weight, profile photo, portfolio photos, team logo, organizer verification document, rating history, notifications, team memberships, and your sign-in credentials. Once deleted your account cannot be recovered, and signing up again will not restore anything.

Retained in anonymized form: match results, statistics and events that involve other people. These records belong to every participant, not only to you — removing them would erase other players' game history and delete events others took part in. Your name is permanently replaced with "Deleted user" in these records, and they no longer identify you.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) you have the right to be informed about how your data is used, to access it, to correct inaccurate information, to object to processing, and to have your data erased. You may exercise these rights by editing your profile in the app, by deleting your account, or by contacting us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

You may also lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines (privacy.gov.ph).

Children

Homegrown is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. The app does not ask for your date of birth. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] and we will remove it.

Security

Access to your data is controlled by server-side security rules that limit what each signed-in user can read and write. Uploaded images are subject to the same controls, and organizer verification documents are restricted to the uploader and an administrator. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.
