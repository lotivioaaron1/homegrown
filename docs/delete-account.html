Delete your Homegrown account

Two ways to permanently remove your account and personal data.

Fastest: delete it yourself in the app

If you still have Homegrown installed, you can delete your account immediately without waiting for anyone:

  1. Open Homegrown and sign in
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Tap Settings
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account
  5. Confirm with your password (or your Google account) and type DELETE

Your account is removed straight away.

If you have uninstalled the app

Email us from the email address registered to your Homegrown account — we use it to confirm the request is really yours:

[YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]

Put "Account deletion request" in the subject line. You do not need to explain why. We will confirm once it is done, normally within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Permanently, with no way to recover it:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your barangay, bio, and — for athletes — height and weight
  • Your profile photo and portfolio photos
  • Your team logo, if you are a coach
  • Your organizer verification document
  • Your rating history, notifications and team memberships
  • Your sign-in credentials — you will not be able to log back in

What stays, and why

Match results, statistics and events that involve other people are kept, with your name permanently replaced by "Deleted user".

These records belong to everyone who took part, not only to you. Deleting a match would erase your opponents' game history too, and deleting an event would remove it for every player who joined. Once anonymized, these records no longer identify you.

Is it reversible?

No. Deletion is permanent and immediate. Creating a new account with the same email address later will not restore any of your previous data, statistics or history.
